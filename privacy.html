Neon Viper

Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

Neon Viper: Arcade (“the app”) is designed to work without an account and without a developer server. This policy explains exactly what is stored and what leaves your device.

Information stored on your device

Your racer tag, high scores, career statistics, unlocked skins and themes, coin balance, achievements, and preferences are stored in local app storage on your device. They are not transmitted to the developer.

Apple Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center and “Game Center ranks” is on, your best score for each mode and your Game Center display name are sent to Apple so they can appear on the global leaderboard. Apple handles that data under its own privacy policy. Neon Viper operates no server of its own and never receives, stores, or shares your Apple ID.

If you are not signed in to Game Center, or you turn rankings off, no score ever leaves your device. Scores already posted to Game Center can be managed or removed through your Apple Game Center settings.

No advertising, analytics, or tracking

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. It does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or Bluetooth. There are no in-app purchases.

Data retention and deletion

Uninstalling the app or using “Reset All Progress” permanently erases your local data. Game Center leaderboard entries are controlled by Apple and can be managed through your Apple account.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There are no accounts, chat features, or user-generated content.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above revised. Material changes will also be described in the app's release notes.
