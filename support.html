Frequently asked questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Your racer tag, scores, coins, and unlocks are stored only on your device. Game Center sign-in is optional.
  • How do I play? Swipe anywhere on the board, use the arrow keys, or turn on the on-screen D-pad in Settings. Eat apples to grow, avoid walls and your tail.
  • Why are leaderboards empty? Open Settings inside the game, turn on Game Center ranks, then sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings.
  • How do I reset my progress? Settings → Reset All Progress. This permanently erases local data and cannot be undone.
  • Does the game work offline? Yes. Gameplay, cosmetics, and career stats work without a network. Only Game Center needs a connection.
  • Are there ads or in-app purchases? No. All coins are earned by playing and have no monetary value.
  • Which devices are supported? iPhone and iPad running a current version of iOS or iPadOS. The game is portrait-only.
