Neon Viper: Arcade

Swipe. Survive. Evolve. A neon snake arcade game for iPhone and iPad.

Neon Viper: Arcade is a fast, offline snake game with three modes — Classic, Portal Zen, and Labyrinth — plus power-ups, unlockable skins, a career progression track, and Game Center leaderboards.

Support

Need help, found a bug, or have a feature request? Email tamimarif03@gmail.com and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Everything is stored locally on your device. Only Apple Game Center is optional.
  • Why are leaderboards empty? Open Settings in the game and make sure Game Center ranks is on, then sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings.
  • How do I reset my progress? Open Settings inside the game and choose Reset All Progress. This permanently erases local scores, coins, and unlocks.
  • Does the game work offline? Yes. All three modes, cosmetics, and progress work without a network connection. Game Center needs a connection.
  • Are there ads or in-app purchases? No. Coins are earned by playing and have no monetary value.
